Terms of use

TrackerTFT is a free tool for Teamfight Tactics players: it shows the current meta compositions, lets you note which compositions the players in your lobby are playing, and shows your history against players you've met. By using TrackerTFT or creating an account you agree to these terms.

Your account

You can create an account with an email and password, with Google, or with your Riot account. Keep your password to yourself; you're responsible for what happens with your account. One person per account, and you must be old enough to consent to online services where you live (at least 13, or older where the law says so).

Every account is linked to one Riot account you own, and each Riot account to only one TrackerTFT account. To prove it's yours we ask you to change your profile icon for a moment, or you sign in with Riot. Until it's linked the account can't use any feature. Don't try to link accounts that aren't yours.

Fair use

Don't abuse the service: no automated scraping, no attempts to get around rate limits or bot checks, no attacks on the site, and no use that breaks Riot Games' policies. We may suspend accounts that do.

Plans

TrackerTFT has a free plan. A paid Premium plan may be offered later; its price and conditions will be shown before you buy it.

No warranty

TrackerTFT is provided as it is. Game data comes from Riot Games and from public community sites, and may be incomplete or late. We aren't liable for decisions you make with it, as far as the law allows.

Changes and ending

We may update these terms; if a change matters, we'll ask you to accept it again. You can delete your account at any time from Account settings.

Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal data TrackerTFT keeps when you have an account, why, and what you can do about it. You can use the lobby tools without an account; then we don't keep anything about you beyond short-lived technical logs.

Public match data from Riot Games (Riot IDs, placements and boards of the games you look up) is stored to show lobby and versus history and to find a player by the name seen in game, as it is for everyone who uses the tool.

Cookies

When you sign in we set one cookie, needed for the account to work: it keeps you signed in for up to 30 days of inactivity, can't be read by the page's scripts, and is only sent to TrackerTFT's account service. A second, short-lived cookie (10 minutes) protects a Google or Riot sign-in while it happens. We use no advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps a few preferences (your region, your favourite comp source).

Who helps us run it

Our server and database run on Oracle Cloud, and the site on Netlify. Sign-up forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to keep bots out. Emails are sent by our email provider. If you choose Google or Riot, they handle that sign-in under their own privacy policies. New passwords are checked against known data breaches through Have I Been Pwned without sending the password: only the first five characters of its SHA-1 hash leave our server.

How long

Your account data stays while you have an account. Expired sessions are deleted automatically, and one-time codes within a day. When you delete your account, everything listed above is deleted right away.

Your rights

You can see and change your username, password and linked accounts in Account settings, and delete your account there. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or anything else about your data, write to tftrackercom@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS); passwords are hashed with Argon2id; sign-ins are rate-limited and repeated failures lock the account for a while; sessions and codes are stored only as hashes.
